About the project

Notice the ordinary.

Stinkpipes let air escape from sewers. This atlas helps people record these details of street history.

A new community record

The atlas starts with the collector’s map, focused on London with locations elsewhere in Britain.

We imported 226 point markers on 24 October 2026: 217 historical pipe reports and nine research notes. Seven river lines were excluded. Every imported record keeps its source label and coordinates. Source identifiers are retained where available. The export supplied no individual placemark identifiers; generated atlas identifiers are labelled as such.

Read the original Stinkpipe Collector post

The imported locations have not been rechecked. Some positions are approximate. Coincident source markers remain separate. Original descriptions and photographs stay on the source map. No public reuse licence has been confirmed; source labels and coordinates are credited here as historical reports. No endorsement is claimed.

What does approval mean?

An owner has reviewed the contribution. Approval does not prove its history or current condition. Records are labelled “Community record”.

Privacy and contributions

Operator

Alex Daish operates Stinkpipe Atlas. Use the contact links on his website for privacy requests. Use the report form for record errors, photo concerns or removal requests.

What becomes public

Approved records display their location name, position, description, photo and chosen credit name. Leave the credit field empty to use “Anonymous contributor”. Do not include personal addresses, faces, number plates, contact details or private access information.

What stays private

Pending submissions, reports and optional email addresses are visible only to the operator. Email is used only to discuss your submission or report. We use a salted hash of your network address for hourly spam limits. We do not store that raw address in our contribution database.

Storage and retention

OpenAI Sites and its Cloudflare infrastructure host the app, database and photo storage. Optional email is removed after 90 days during the next routine maintenance run. Unapproved submissions and their unused photos are removed on the same basis. Approved records remain until changed or removed by the operator. Recovery snapshots remain for 30 days, so deleted details can remain in backups until those snapshots expire.

Photos

Your browser resizes photos and converts them to JPEG. The server removes JPEG application and comment metadata, including EXIF location metadata. Source photographs from Stinkpipe Collector have not been copied.

Contribution permission

Submit only material you own or can license. New text and photos use Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Others may reuse them with appropriate credit and a licence link. This permission cannot normally be withdrawn for copies already shared under the licence. We can still remove material from this website. Imported source records retain their separate source credit and rights status.

Community profiles and votes

Sign-in is needed to save favourite votes and link new discoveries to your profile. Choose an alias before you contribute for points. Public leaderboard participation is optional. Only approved new discoveries can earn a point; imported records and corrections do not count. Discovery credit is limited to one report within a 30 m neighbourhood in this beta, so separate nearby pipes may be undercounted. We store your sign-in identifier privately to prevent duplicate votes and attribute contributions. Your email, sign-in identifier and individual favourite choices are not published. Aggregate vote counts are public. Hide your profile to remove your public alias and ranking. Contact the operator if you want your account data removed.

External services and browser storage

OpenStreetMap supplies map tiles. Google Fonts supplies fonts. These services receive ordinary connection information when your browser requests their content. Your browser stores a private receipt for your latest submission. If you choose sign-in during a contribution, this tab temporarily keeps the text draft for up to two hours. Photos and private contact email are not included in that draft. Owner sign-in uses ChatGPT and its essential sign-in storage. We have not added advertising or analytics.

Historical reports

Historical locations and present condition are unverified. Do not enter private property or approach traffic to inspect a pipe. This project is independent of Stinkpipe Collector.
